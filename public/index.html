👑 Maroon Isles Quest

A parody game, built as a proof of concept for two-player co-operative text adventures.

Hair Today, Bald Tomorrow. Prince Alexander has been shipwrecked on the Isle of the Crown. At dawn, the treacherous Vizier Alhazred marries Princess Cassima and seizes the Maroon Isles. Two of you, one night, one chance. You are put into the first quest with a part going, and whichever of the two is free is yours.
